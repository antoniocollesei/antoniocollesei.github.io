I'm a researcher in Bioinformatics and Computational Oncology at the Veneto Instute of Oncology, Italy, and previously Ph.D student (2020-2023) under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Vandin ([Vandin Lab](https://www.dei.unipd.it/~vandinfa/people.html)). I am currently under part-time agreement with the University of Padova, collaborating with Silvio Bicciato's Lab.

I received my Master's Degree in Bioengineering from the University of Padova in 2017, including a one-year visiting research period at the Medizinische Universität, Innsbruck, Austria, under the supervision of Zlatko Trajanoski and [Francesca Finotello](https://computationalbiomedicinegroup.github.io/). Between my Master's and Ph.D. I temporarily switched to industry, working for an italian multinational company ([Inventis](http://www.inventis.it/world)).

My research mainly focuses on the development of ML and AI methods for omics data, mainly single cell and spatial transcriptomics. I have been lately presenting my work at RECOMB, ISMB-ECCB, CIBB, and ECML-PKDD.
