I am a Bioinformatics and Computational Oncology researcher at the Veneto Institute of Oncology (IOV-IRCCS) and an affiliated researcher with Silvio Bicciato’s Lab at the University of Padova. My research centers on building machine learning and AI algorithms for single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, with recent work presented at RECOMB, ISMB-ECCB, CIBB, and ECML-PKDD.

I hold a Ph.D. (2020–2023) from the University of Padova, under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Vandin ([Vandin Lab](https://www.dei.unipd.it/~vandinfa/people.html)), and an M.Sc. in Bioengineering (2017) from the same university. My background also includes a one-year visiting research fellowship at the Medical University of Innsbruck with Zlatko Trajanoski and [Francesca Finotello](https://computationalbiomedicinegroup.github.io/), as well as industry experience at the Italian multinational company [Inventis](http://www.inventis.it/world).
